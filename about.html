Who We Are

About Us

A California nonprofit dedicated to the cats no one else is reaching — living quietly in the margins of every highway corridor.

Our Mission

Every cat deserves a stable, healthy life

Roadcat Rescue is a California nonprofit dedicated to humanely managing feral cat populations at highway rest stops through Trap-Neuter-Return. We believe every cat deserves a stable, healthy life — even those living in the margins of our highway system, invisible to the world rushing past at 70 miles per hour.

The Problem

A hidden crisis on the open road

California's highway rest stops are home to hundreds of feral cat colonies — populations of cats who have lived outdoors for generations, never socialized to human contact, and never able to be adopted. These are not lost pets. They are wild animals who have adapted to rest stop life.

But that life is brutal. Hemmed in by fast-moving traffic on all sides, they cannot safely disperse. Without intervention, a single colony of 10 cats can grow to 80–100 in two years. They face dehydration in California's inland heat, predation, disease, and the constant risk of vehicle strike.

Most die before the age of five. Without TNR, the cycle never ends.

The Gap

Why rest stop cats are uniquely underserved

These animals fall through every crack in the system — by geography, by jurisdiction, by oversight.

🗺️

Geographic isolation

Urban and suburban TNR organizations serve their local communities. Highway rest stops — often miles from the nearest city — fall outside anyone's service area.

🏛️

Jurisdictional complexity

Rest stops are state-managed land under Caltrans. Most nonprofits lack the relationships or legal framework to operate on state right-of-way. We're building that framework.

🚫

Shelter ineligibility

Traditional animal shelters cannot accept feral cats — animals that have never been socialized cannot be adopted. TNR is the only evidence-based humane solution for these populations.

How We Work

TNR + colony management + partnership

Our approach has three integrated pillars that work together to create lasting, humane population management rather than a temporary fix.

  • Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) — the evidence-based gold standard for feral cat population management, proven in hundreds of communities worldwide.
  • Ongoing colony monitoring — each colony gets a dedicated volunteer manager who checks in regularly, provides food and water in extreme weather, and documents population trends.
  • Caltrans partnership — we work directly with the California Department of Transportation to operate legally on state right-of-way, ensuring our work is sanctioned, safe, and sustainable.

We don't believe in one-time interventions. A colony TNR'd once, then forgotten, will recover in population as new cats drift in. Colony management is the commitment that makes TNR work long-term.
